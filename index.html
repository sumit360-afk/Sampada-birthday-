Chapter two · a note for you

A little letter

Dear Sampada,

Happy Birthday! ❤️

I wanted to make you something that feels a little more personal than a regular birthday wish. So here's a small corner of the internet, made with a few memories, a little stardust, and lots of good wishes for you.

It's funny how the simplest things can become memorable—the random talks, the silly teasing, the little updates, and those moments when a quick check-in makes the day feel warmer. I hope you know that these little moments are appreciated.

As you step into another year, I hope life gives you more reasons to smile, more places to explore, more dreams that turn into plans, and more beautiful surprises than you expect. Keep making that bucket list longer—and one day, tick off those trips one by one.

And whenever you feel like you need someone, I hope you know you can reach out to me. On the happy days, the tiring ones, or the days when you cannot quite find the words—I will always make room to listen. You do not have to pretend that everything is okay around me.

If I could give you one little gift beyond this page, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes, even for a moment. Maybe then you would understand how wonderfully you shine, how much goodness you carry, and how special the little things about you really are. I wish you could see the version of you that I get to see.

You do not have to be anything other than yourself to make this world a little more beautiful.

Here's to your happiness, your dreams, your adventures, and all the lovely little things still waiting for you.

With lots of warmth,
Someone cheering for you ✨
